For Windows

Your words. Your workspace.

Keyword Office is a Windows desktop word processor for creating and editing documents, with support for common document formats and Google Docs integration.

Read our privacy policy

Work on your desktop

Write and edit documents in a native Windows application. Work with common formats including Word documents (DOCX), Rich Text Format (RTF), and plain text (TXT).

Google sign-in is used for Google integration. You can work with local documents without connecting a Google account.

Connect to Google Docs

Connect your Google account to find and open Google documents, save changes, synchronize supported edits, and manage document collaborators.

The current integration requests broad Google Drive access to work with existing documents and Google Drive shortcuts. Review the permission details and local storage information before connecting.
